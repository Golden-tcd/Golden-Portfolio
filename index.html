About

From gears to code, with a love for craft.

I grew up wanting to be a mechanical engineer, fascinated by inventions and the thrill of making something that didn't exist yesterday. That curiosity found a new home when I started asking how computers, phones and apps actually work.

Today I'm a Computer Science student at the Nigerian Army University, Biu, focused on frontend development. Outside code, I design graphics and produce beats, which keeps me thinking about both how things work and how they feel to use.
